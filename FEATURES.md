# Idea Roulette: features and add-ons

## Shipped in v1

- **Casino wheel** with 16 slices, ticking pointer, blinking rim bulbs and a 5-second eased spin.
- **Fortune cookie reveal**: the cookie drops in, wobbles, cracks, and a paper slip unfolds with the idea.
- **400 ideas** in 8 categories: Money Maker, Change the World, Absurd & Funny, Dev Tools, Games & Toys, AI Weirdness, Everyday Life, Creative & Art.
- **40 twists** (🌶️ Add a twist) that stack onto any idea: 16,400 possible fortunes.
- **Copy build prompt**: a ready-to-paste prompt for Claude, v0, Lovable, Bolt, Cursor.
- **Share link**: every fortune has its own URL (`/#money-9-5`) that opens straight to the cookie.
- **Category filter**: switch categories off and the wheel greys them out.
- **Past fortunes**: the last 12 spins, stored in your browser.
- Sound effects with a mute toggle, Space to spin, reduced-motion support, works on phones.

## Next up (small, high delight)

1. **Daily Fortune**: one shared idea per day for everyone, with a countdown. Gives people a reason to come back.
2. **"I built it!" gallery**: submit a link to what you built from your fortune; show a wall of builds per idea.
3. **Difficulty dial**: Easy / Weekend / Ambitious changes which ideas can land.
4. **Stack filter**: "no backend", "needs AI", "needs payments", so a one-shot is realistic.
5. **Jackpot mode**: rare golden slice (1 in 50) that serves a legendary idea with extra flair.
6. **Slot-machine mode**: three reels (WHO × WHAT × TWIST) for combinatorial chaos.
7. **Reroll tokens**: three rerolls per day, so the spin still feels like it matters.
8. **Open-in buttons**: send the prompt straight to v0, Lovable, Bolt or Claude with one click.
9. **Downloadable fortune card**: a PNG of the paper slip for sharing on X and Instagram.
10. **Dynamic OG images**: shared links preview the actual idea, not a generic card.

## Community and growth

11. **Upvote ideas**: rank ideas by how many people want them built.
12. **Submit your own ideas** to the wheel, with moderation.
13. **Hackathon mode**: a room code gives everyone in a group the same fortune, with a timer.
14. **Challenge a friend**: send a sealed fortune; it only opens when they accept the challenge.
15. **Streaks**: build one fortune a week and keep your streak alive.
16. **Leaderboard of builders**: most fortunes shipped this month.
17. **Embed widget**: a tiny wheel other sites and newsletters can embed.
18. **Discord / Slack bot**: `/fortune` spins the wheel in chat.
19. **Weekly newsletter**: the five most-built ideas and the best builds.

## Deeper product

20. **AI-generated ideas**: an "Infinite" category where an LLM invents a fresh idea every spin.
21. **Idea expander**: turn a fortune into a mini spec: user stories, data model, pages.
22. **Market check**: "does this already exist?" search with a quick verdict.
23. **Revenue calculator** for Money Maker ideas: price × users = rent paid?
24. **Starter repos**: one-click template per category (Next.js, Vite, Expo).
25. **Build timer**: a one-shot stopwatch with a "ship it" moment and confetti.
26. **Localization**: fortunes in Swedish, Spanish, Japanese and more.
27. **Themes**: neon Vegas, Monte Carlo classic, Chinese new year, retro arcade.
28. **Haptics on phones** when the pointer clicks past each slice.
29. **Accounts (optional)**: sync history and builds across devices.
30. **Pro tier (optional, still free core)**: private idea packs for agencies and teachers.
