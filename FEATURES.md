# Idea Roulette: features and add-ons

## Shipped

- **Casino wheel** with 16 slices, ticking pointer, blinking rim bulbs and a 5-second eased spin.
- **Fortune cookie reveal**: the cookie drops in, wobbles, cracks, and a paper slip unfolds with the idea.
- **451 ideas** in 8 categories: Money Maker, Change the World, Absurd & Funny, Dev Tools, Games & Toys, AI Weirdness, Everyday Life, Creative & Art.
- **40 twists** (🌶️ Add a twist) that stack onto any idea.
- **Copy build prompt**: a ready-to-paste prompt for Claude, v0, Lovable, Bolt, Cursor.
- **Share link**: every fortune has its own URL (`/#money-9-5`) that opens straight to the cookie.
- **Category filter** and **stack filter** (no backend · uses AI · takes payments · uses device hardware), so a one-shot is realistic. The wheel refuses to spin into an empty combination.
- **Jackpot mode**: a 1 in 40 spin serves one of 25 legendary ideas, with gold bulbs, a banner, a golden slip and triple confetti.
- **Daily fortune**: one shared idea per UTC day for everyone, with a countdown (`#daily`).
- **Challenge a friend**: a sealed envelope link with a deadline; decline and you get the chicken 🐔.
- **Themes**: Monte Carlo, Neon Vegas, Lunar New Year, Retro Arcade.
- **Downloadable fortune card**: a 1200x630 PNG of the fortune, drawn on a canvas, shared through the phone's share sheet where possible.
- **Upvote ideas** (▲) and a 🔥 Most wanted list.
- **"I built it!" gallery**: post a link to what you built under a nickname you claim with a recovery code, no login. Per-idea galleries and a recent-builds wall. Reports auto-hide abuse, the owner can unhide.
- **Builder leaderboard** (this month and all time), **weekly streaks** (🔥 3 weeks: build at least once a week to keep it), and **builder pages** (`#builder-<nick>`) with a builder's builds, streak and totals.
- **Past fortunes**: the last 12 spins, stored in your browser.
- Sound effects with a mute toggle, Space to spin, reduced-motion support, works on phones.

## Next up (small, high delight)

2. **Slot-machine mode**: three reels (WHO × WHAT × TWIST) for combinatorial chaos.
3. **Reroll tokens**: three rerolls per day, so the spin still feels like it matters.

5. **Dynamic OG images**: shared links preview the actual idea, not a generic card.

## Community and growth

8. **Embed widget**: a tiny wheel other sites and newsletters can embed.
9. **Discord / Slack bot**: `/fortune` spins the wheel in chat.
10. **Weekly newsletter**: the five most-built ideas and the best builds.
